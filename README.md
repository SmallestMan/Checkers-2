This is The First Mostly Playable Version of The Game

Controls: 
- Left Click to Select Piece
Left Click Again to Move it
- Space to Buy a Random Card For a Flat $1000
- Left Click on Roulette Text to Open Roulette Menu
- Drag Around Slider to Change Bet
- C to Spin Roulette (Don't Ask Why Its C)

There's no Main Menu, so You Have to Alt+F4 to Close The Game
There's no Multiplayer yet, so You Kinda Have to Play With Yourself ;)
